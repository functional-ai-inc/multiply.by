== App name [30 characters] ==
Multiply.by – Times Tables

== Short description [80 characters] ==
Learn the times tables with free games: no ads, no sign-up, works offline.

== Full description [4000 characters] ==
Multiply.by is a quick, focused way for children to learn the multiplication table.

Each question shows one fact, such as 7 x 8, and four answers to tap. A fact counts as learned
only when it is answered right: a missed one comes back near the end, until every fact is
learned. After the game, a short practice round can bring back the facts that were hardest.

Pick a game:
- Monster: feed a hungry monster and help it grow.
- Robot: build a robot, part by part.
- Trains: travel from steam engines to maglev trains.
- Dinosaurs: meet a new dinosaur as you learn.
- Planes: the history of flight.
- Space: a journey through the solar system.
- Chinese: build the Great Wall of China.
- Block Craft: mine and build with blocks.
- Evolution: life on Earth, from the first cells on.
- Buildings: from the Pyramids to the Shanghai Tower.
- The base game: the plain multiplication table, no extras.
There is a division game too.

Tables 2 to 9, both ways (3 x 4 and 4 x 3), up to 12 or up to 20: choose under RULES.

For parents and teachers:
- Free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions.
- No sign-up and no account. The app collects no data; progress stays on the phone.
- Works offline: every game is inside the app.
- Links that leave the app (the website, sharing) ask a grown-up to solve a multiplication
  question first.

Built by AI, open to everyone:
Every game was built by an AI model from a prompt of a few lines. The prompt is one tap away,
under SOURCE in every game. On the website, multiply.by, you can see every game's prompt and
build your own game about whatever your child loves, with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. No
programming needed.

Multiply.by is open source (MIT License) by Functional AI Inc.
