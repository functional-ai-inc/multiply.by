# Privacy policy

For the Multiply.by app for Android (`by.multiply.app`).
The website https://multiply.by has its own policy: https://multiply.by/privacy/website/.
Effective 21 November 2026.

Multiply.by is made by Functional AI Inc. for children learning the times tables. We collect no
personal information from anyone, children included.

## The app

- **No data leaves the phone.** All the games are inside the app; it loads nothing from the
  internet while you play.
- **No accounts, no sign-up**: the app never asks for a name, email address, age or any other
  personal information.
- **No ads, no analytics, no tracking**: the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking
  code, and no code from other companies that collects data.
- **Progress stays on the phone**: scores and learned facts are saved only on the device, in the
  app's own storage, so a game can continue where it stopped. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its
  data in Android's settings, deletes them. Android's own device backup may include them, under
  your Google account's backup settings; we never receive them.
- **No permissions beyond the basics**: the app does not use the camera, microphone, location,
  contacts or files.
- **Leaving the app**: links to the website, to other sites and to sharing first ask a grown-up to
  answer a multiplication question. A link then opens in the phone's browser, and sharing opens
  Android's share menu; what happens after that is up to that browser or app and its own policy.

## Children

The app is made for children and collect no personal information from them (or
from anyone). There is nothing to share with third parties, and nothing is sold.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
