# Website privacy policy

For the website https://multiply.by. Effective 22 November 2026.
The Android app has its own policy: https://multiply.by/privacy/.

Multiply.by is made by Functional AI Inc. for children learning the times tables. The site has no
accounts and never asks for a name, email address, age or any other personal information.

## The games

- **No ads in the games**: the game pages (the front page, /base/ and every game's own page) show
  no ads.
- **Progress stays in your browser**: scores and learned facts are saved only in your browser's
  local storage, so a game can continue where it stopped. Clearing the browser's site data deletes
  them; we never receive them.
- **No analytics, no tracking cookies**: the site uses no analytics or tracking code, and the
  pages load nothing from other companies' servers (no outside fonts, scripts or pictures).

## Ads on other pages

Pages other than the games may show ads, chosen by who each page is for:

- **Pages for children and families** (for example About): the ads are contextual, chosen by the
  page they are on, never personalized or based on a visitor's interests or other sites they
  visited. We tell the ad network that these pages are directed to children, so it treats every
  visitor as a child under its own rules (for Google: child-directed treatment under COPPA), with
  no interest-based ads and no remarketing.
- **Build It** (https://multiply.by/build-it/), a page for adults about building games with AI
  models: the ads may be personalized, based on the visitor's interests as the ad network knows
  them. In the EU, the UK and other places where the law requires it, personalized ads are shown
  only after the visitor agrees in a consent message, and the choice can be changed at any time;
  without consent the ads are contextual. Ad categories unsuitable for children (such as dating,
  gambling and alcohol) are blocked on every page, this one included.
- On every page with ads, the ad network may use cookies or similar storage, and receives the
  visitor's IP address and browser details, to show the ad, limit how often it is shown, count
  views and prevent fraud. Its own privacy policy covers this.

We do not see who views or clicks an ad, and we receive no personal information from the ad
network.

## Server logs

Like any web server, ours keeps a standard access log: the time of each request, the page asked
for, the visitor's IP address and the browser's name. We use it only to keep the site running and
secure (our legitimate interest), never to identify or track visitors; we never share or sell it,
and each log is deleted after 14 days.

## Children

The games are made for children; the site collects no personal information from them (or from
anyone) beyond the server log and what an ad network receives as described above. Personalized ads
appear only on Build It, the page for adults. Links that leave the site open other sites with
their own policies.

## Your rights

Visitors in the EU, the UK and elsewhere may have the right to ask what data about them we hold, and
to have it corrected or deleted. Since we hold nothing but server logs, which we cannot link to a
person, there is usually nothing to give or delete; write to us and we will check.
